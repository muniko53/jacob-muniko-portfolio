Privacy

Privacy, plainly.

This site is a portfolio, not an ad business. Here is what it stores, what it sends, and what it does not do.

No advertising trackers. No cookie banner. Fonts are served from this site.

Browser storage

Your theme choice stays in your browser.

The only thing this site stores in your browser is your light or dark theme preference, under the key jm-theme in localStorage. It is read before the page paints so the theme does not flash. It is not sent anywhere.

You can remove it by clearing site data for this domain in your browser settings. The site will return to the dark default.

Contact

Your inquiry is used to reply, not to build a list.

The inquiry form asks for your name, email, project type, an optional budget and timeline, and your message. I use those details only to understand the project and reply. They are not added to a mailing list, sold, or shared for marketing.

If the direct form service is enabled, Web3Forms forwards the message to my email address. If it is not enabled, the form opens your email app with the message filled in. This site does not keep a separate database of inquiries. I keep inquiry emails only as long as needed to respond and manage the work, and you can ask me to delete an inquiry at any time.

Analytics

No advertising trackers. No cross-site profiles.

This site does not currently run analytics. If privacy-friendly analytics are enabled later, they will be cookieless and aggregate: page counts and referrers, not individual profiles. There are no advertising pixels, social embeds, or third-party video players on the site.

Fonts are served from this site, not from a third-party font host. The only third-party requests are the ones you choose by following a link to Linklet, GitHub, or WhatsApp.

External links

Other sites have their own rules.

This site links to Linklet, GitHub, and WhatsApp. Those services have their own privacy policies and data practices. This site does not control them.

Your choices

Ask, delete, or use another route.

You can email jacobmuniko53@gmail.com to ask what inquiry details I hold or to ask me to delete them. You can also skip the form entirely and use email or WhatsApp.
