About

I build for the part after the demo.

A demo only has to survive the person who built it. A real system has to survive a slow connection, a user who does the unexpected thing, a model that runs out of quota, and a folder full of files nobody meant to touch.

I am in my final year of a Bachelor of Education (Arts) at Karatina University, combining Business Studies and Geography. That background shaped how I scope work: understand the people and the constraint before choosing the stack. I expect to graduate around November 2027.

I use AI coding tools as part of my process. I define the architecture, review the changes, test the behaviour, and stay accountable for what ships. The tools speed up the work. They do not get the credit or the blame.
